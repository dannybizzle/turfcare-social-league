Weekly Scheduling

The current week and next week become official automatically. The next unpublished week is a live projection that changes as availability or the roster changes. No administrator publishing is needed.

Choose a season to view its schedule.
How automatic scheduling works
Season defaults: Five sports (Ping Pong, Darts, Pool, Air Hockey and Beer Pong) · Three different sports per matchup · Two wins take the matchup · Total game wins break ties · Weekly deadline 11:59 PM (according to the schedule) · Fair rotating byes · Playing ahead allowed.

The automatic publisher locks the current and following week. The next unpublished week is shown as a live, changing projection. A projection is not an official matchup: opponents and assigned sports may change before publication.
